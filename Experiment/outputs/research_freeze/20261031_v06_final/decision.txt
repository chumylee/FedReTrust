FINAL PROJECT RESEARCH DECISION — 31 October2026 (Perth)

Selected primary method: FedReTrust v0.6.
Keep v0.5 as the important earlier-version comparison, particularly for IID.
The implementation remains version0.6-exploratory; selection does not imply production readiness.

Reason for selection
The project seeks a useful protection/adaptation compromise without clean server reference data.
v0.6 improves non-IID poisoning protection over v0.5 in every paired block and retains better
honest-stripe accuracy than RFA. This is a qualitative project choice, not a universal ranking.

Five-block evidence (v0.5 -> v0.6)
Non-IID label-flip damage:7.3835 ->5.6152 percentage points.
Paired reduction:1.7684 points; nominal95% interval[0.6225,2.9142].
Non-IID absolute attacked accuracy:77.3488% ->79.1980%.
Non-IID honest-stripe accuracy:80.3113% ->80.8128%; the gain is uncertain across blocks.
IID honest-stripe accuracy:82.0407% ->80.9232%.
Paired loss:1.1176 points; nominal95% interval[0.0148,2.2203].
IID clean/noise metrics are unchanged; the IID attack-damage difference is small and uncertain.

RFA remains stronger on average non-IID protection:4.6578 points damage and81.2872% attacked
accuracy. v0.6 has higher non-IID stripe accuracy:80.8128% versus77.4730% for RFA.
All intervals are exploratory paired t intervals across five known development blocks.

Unmet targets
45/51 follow-up checks passed. The six failures are IID mean stripe accuracy relative to FedAvg,
non-IID mean noise accuracy relative to FedAvg, and IID stripe retention in blocks1,2,4,5.
All earlier pilot decisions remain unchanged. No all-target success is claimed.

Frozen scope
v0.6 covers clean training, honest noise, honest stripes and persistent label flipping,
with IID/non-IID, five existing blocks and24 rounds. It does not establish recovery after
attack cessation, recurring/mixed/adaptive attack robustness, partial participation or new datasets.
No proof of malicious intent, universal superiority, established novelty, differential privacy,
secure aggregation or production readiness is claimed.

Research is frozen for reporting
Preserve the existing algorithms, parameters, seeds, partitions, protocols, raw results and failures.
No further variants, tuning or performance experiments are scheduled.
Any future scientific amendment requires a separately documented, explicitly authorized change.
Code cleanup should use a separate submission copy and demonstrate preserved behaviour.
Reproduction checks and demos must remain separately labelled and must not overwrite study results.
New tables, figures, prose and presentation material may be derived from the saved artifacts.
